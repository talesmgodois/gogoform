Everything a form needs, nothing to host {{helpIcon "how-it-works"}}

  • Visual form builder

    Drag fields onto the canvas: text, choices, dates, ratings and file uploads, with required fields and validation.

  • Public pages and embeds

    Every form gets a link to share and can be embedded in your site, or submitted to from your own frontend through the API.

  • Responses and files in one place

    Browse submissions on the dashboard with their uploaded files, and schedule forms to open and close on their own.

  • Webhooks and exports

    Send each submission to your systems as it arrives, or download all of them as CSV or JSON.

  • Email notifications

    Get an email for every new response, so nothing waits in the inbox of a dashboard.

  • Spam protection

    Honeypot fields, timing checks, rate limits and optional Cloudflare Turnstile keep bots out.
