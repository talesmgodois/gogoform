Privacy policy

1. Account data

When you sign up we store your email address, a hash of your password and your workspace settings, to run your account and send you service emails such as address verification, password resets and payment notices.

2. Form responses

Responses and files submitted to a form are stored on behalf of the form owner, who decides what the form collects and what happens to the responses. We act as a processor for that data: if you responded to a form, contact its owner about your data. Along with a response we keep technical metadata such as the IP address and user agent, for spam protection.

3. Payments

Payments for the Pro plan are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers; we only keep the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and the subscription status. See Stripe's privacy policy for how Stripe handles your data.

4. Other processors

We use an email delivery provider to send service emails and, when enabled on a form, Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection.

5. Cookies

We only set the session cookie that keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account from your account settings cancels your subscription and deletes your workspace, forms, responses and files; database backups containing them expire within a few weeks.
