# Pricing

The pricing page compares the **Free** and **Pro** plans. The figures shown
are the limits GoGoForm actually enforces.

## Plans

- **Free** is for trying GoGoForm out: a few forms, a monthly submission
  quota and a small file storage. Public forms show a "Powered by GoGoForm"
  badge.
- **Pro** raises the limits and adds webhooks, CSV and JSON exports, an email
  per submission and removes the badge.

## Upgrading

Create an account and open [Billing](/app/billing) to upgrade. Payment is
handled by Stripe; you can cancel at any time from the customer portal.
