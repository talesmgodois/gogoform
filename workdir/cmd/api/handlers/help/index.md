# GoGoForm manual

GoGoForm lets you build a form, share its link and collect the responses, with
no server of your own. This manual explains every page of the app.

## Getting help on any page

Every page has a **?** button in its top bar: it opens this help panel on the
right, showing the manual page of the screen you are on. Smaller **?** icons
next to a feature open the panel at the section explaining that feature. Press
**Esc** or the close button to hide the panel, and use
**Open in the manual** to read the page full screen.

## How it works

1. [Create an account](/app/signin?mode=signup): you get your own workspace.
2. Build a form in the [form builder](/app/builder).
3. Share its public link, or embed it in your website.
4. Read, filter and export the submissions from the form's page.
