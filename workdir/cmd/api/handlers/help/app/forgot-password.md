# Forgot password

Enter the email address of your account and we send you a link to choose a
new password.

## Reset link

For your privacy, the page always says the email was sent, even when no
account uses that address. The link expires after a while and works once;
request a new one if it no longer works. Check your spam folder if the email
does not arrive.
