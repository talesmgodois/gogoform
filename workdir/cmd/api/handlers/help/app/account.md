# Account

Your account settings: your sign-in details, your workspace and its API key.

## Change email

Enter the new address and your current password. We send a verification link
to the new address; it stays unverified until you open it.

## Change password

Enter your current password, then the new one twice (8 to 72 characters).
Reset links you requested before stop working.

## API key

The key lets your servers call the GoGoForm API on behalf of your workspace:
send it in the `X-API-Key` header. **Reveal** shows it, **Copy** copies it and
**Rotate** replaces it with a new one; the old key stops working at once, so
update your servers. Keep it secret.

## Delete account

Deletes your account for good: it cancels your subscription right away and
removes your workspace with all its forms, responses and files. Type the
confirmation text shown to enable the button. This cannot be undone.
