# Your forms

The dashboard of your workspace: your plan usage, your forms and the files
uploaded through them.

## New form

**New form** opens the [form builder](/app/help?doc=app/builder) on an empty
form.

## Plan usage

Shows how much of your plan you use: the number of forms, the submissions
received this month (reset on the 1st of each month, UTC) and the storage
used by uploaded files. A bar turns amber at 80% and red when the limit is
reached; past a limit, new forms, submissions or uploads are refused.
**Upgrade to Pro** raises the limits.

## Forms

Lists your forms, newest first, with their status:

- **Live**: active and inside its schedule, so it accepts submissions.
- **Scheduled**: active, but its start date is in the future.
- **Ended**: active, but its end date has passed.
- **Inactive**: switched off; the public link shows a closed message.

and who can fill them in:

- **Public**: anyone with the link.
- **Private · anonymous**: signed-in users, not recorded.
- **Private · identified**: signed-in users, recorded with each submission.

Open a form to see its submissions.

## Form actions

Each row offers **Edit** (in the builder), **Copy public link**,
**Activate**/**Deactivate** and **Delete**. Forms that already have
submissions or webhooks cannot be deleted; deactivate them instead.

## Files

The files respondents uploaded through your forms, with their size and form.
Click a file to view it. Files count towards your storage limit.
