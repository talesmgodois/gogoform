# Billing

Your workspace's plan and subscription.

## Current plan

Shows whether the workspace is on the **Free** or **Pro** plan, the
subscription status as reported by Stripe and its renewal date. Compare the
plans on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Upgrade

**Upgrade to Pro** opens a secure Stripe checkout. The plan changes as soon
as Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds.

## Manage subscription

**Manage subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal, where you can
update your payment method, download invoices or cancel. When a subscription
ends the workspace goes back to the Free plan; your forms and responses are
kept.
