# Sign in or create an account

Use this page to sign in to your workspace or to create a new account.

## Sign in

Enter your **email or username** and your **password**. After signing in you
are taken back to the page you were trying to open, or to your forms.

## Create account

Switch to **Create account**, then choose a username, your email and a
password of 8 to 72 characters. Every new account gets its own workspace and
the BASIC role; an admin can grant other roles.

We send you an email to verify your address: until it is verified you can
build forms but not publish them.

## Forgot password

Use **Forgot password?** to receive a link to choose a new password. See
[Forgot password](/app/help?doc=app/forgot-password).
